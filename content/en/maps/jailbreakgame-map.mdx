## The Prison: Breaking Free and Surviving

Every criminal begins their journey locked within the Prison complex. Surviving the early game means mastering the structural layout of the facility and knowing which escape corridor offers the least resistance.

```
   [ Police Station / Security ]
                 |
     [ Cells ] - [ Main Cafeteria ] - [ Yard ]
                 |                        |
            [ Sewers ]               [ Exploding Wall ]
                 |                        |
         (River / Outer Gate)      (Prison Gun Shop)
```

### Primary Escape Routes

1. **The Sewer Corridors**: The most reliable path for beginners. Dropping through the sewer grates in the yard or cafeteria takes you beneath the security fences, popping you out near the drainage canal.
2. **The Exploding Wall**: Positioned behind the Prison Gun Shop. Players can trade a donut to Rusty to receive C4 explosives, blowing a direct breach through the concrete wall.
3. **The Air Vents**: Accessible from cell blocks and indoor corridors, allowing agile prisoners to bypass locked security gates without needing a keycard.
4. **Keycard Tramways**: Pickpocketing a police officer unlocks direct access through the Visitors Building and interior security offices, leading directly to the surface vehicle lots.

| Escape Route | Required Gear | Escape Speed | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sewer Pipes** | None | Moderate | Low |
| **Exploding Wall** | Donut / C4 | Instantaneous breach | Medium (Sound attracts cops) |
| **Ceiling Vents** | None | Slow (Crouching required) | Low to Moderate |
| **Front Gate Keycard** | Stolen Keycard | Very Fast | High (Direct cop confrontation) |

## Major Heist Centers Across the Jailbreakgame Map

Once free, your primary objective shifts to acquiring cash. The layout of the **jailbreakgame map** features several major heist targets distributed across two primary metropolitan centers: Rising City and Crown City.

```
   [ Rising City Hub ]                [ Crown City Hub ]
   ├── Bank (Vault heists)            ├── Crown Jewel (Rooftop/Nightclub)
   ├── Jewelry Store (Laser grid)     ├── Grocery Store (Quick cash register)
   ├── Museum (Puzzle artifacts)      └── Penthouse & Luxury Garages
   └── Crime Port (Cargo delivery)
```

### Rising City: The Classic Criminal Playground

Rising City remains the traditional heart of robbery operations. Navigating the towering skyline requires pinpoint driving and mastery of back alleys.

* **Rising City Bank**: A multi-vault facility requiring a keycard. Robbers must crack the safe while defending against SWAT breaches through narrow corridors.
* **The Jewelry Store**: Demands careful rooftop entry, dodging lethal laser security grids, and breaking display cases before leaping off the roof with a glider or parachute.
* **The Museum**: A cooperative heist where two players must coordinate lever pulls, disable security pressure plates, and haul heavy artifacts back to the Volcano Base.
* **Crime Port & Docks**: Ideal for oceanic retreats, serving as vehicle depots and secondary drop-offs.

### Crown City: The Modern Metropolis

Introduced to diversify gameplay, Crown City adds modern architectural challenges, multi-level structures, and unique cash opportunities.

* **The Crown Jewel**: A premier robbery location featuring a nightclub atmosphere and elevator shafts. It challenges players to escape vertically while avoiding high-rise sniper fire.
* **The Grocery Store**: A low-risk, rapid-turnaround heist ideal for solo players looking to pick up pocket cash without facing complex laser grids.
* **Crown City Garages & Penthouses**: Perfect tactical rest stops to repair damaged vehicles, swap modifications, or evade police helicopters.

| Robbery Location | City Sector | Keycard Required? | Difficulty | Escape Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rising City Bank** | Rising City | Yes | High | Smoke grenades & armored vehicle |
| **Jewelry Store** | Rising City | No | Medium | Parachute from roof to nearby supercar |
| **Museum** | Rising City Outskirts | No | Medium | Aerial extraction (Helicopter/Rope) |
| **Crown Jewel** | Crown City | No | High | Elevator descent to high-speed bike |
| **Grocery Store** | Crown City | No | Very Low | Fast street car through alleyways |

## Outskirts, Desert Dunes, and Specialized Facilities

Leaving the urban centers leads into the expansive deserts, jagged canyons, and oceanic waters that make up the rest of the **jailbreakgame map**. These remote zones house some of the most lucrative, time-sensitive activities in the game.

### High-Profile Rural Targets

1. **The Power Plant**: Located in the expansion valley, this puzzle-based heist awards cash based on how quickly you deliver the uranium core to the Volcano Base. Every second spent driving decreases your payout.
2. **The Tomb**: Situated deep within the desert dunes. It requires three players to open, challenging squads to dodge rolling boulders, dart traps, and spike pits to secure ancient gems.
3. **The Military Base**: Heavily guarded with automated anti-aircraft turrets and forcefields. Infiltrating this base allows players to commandeer military-grade vehicles like attack helicopters and tanks.
4. **The Oil Rig**: Anchored far out at sea, this complex oceanic structure demands tactical watercraft or aerial approaches, testing players with rising platform security and automated defense systems.

| Outskirts Facility | Best Approach Vehicle | Primary Objective | Safety Hazard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Power Plant** | Volt Bike / Roadster | Quick circuit puzzle | Decaying payout timer |
| **The Tomb** | Dune Buggy / Helicopter | Co-op platforming | Rolling boulders & spikes |
| **Military Base** | Blackhawk / Tank | Vehicle theft & munitions | Automated missile turrets |
| **Oil Rig** | Speedboat / Little Bird | Maritime infiltration | Open water & aerial snipers |

## Optimal Robbery Routes for Criminals

To maximize your hourly earnings across the **jailbreakgame map**, running random heists is not enough. You need an optimized rotation that capitalizes on opening cycles while minimizing travel downtime.

### Route A: The Rapid Urban Loop (Solo Friendly)

* **Step 1**: Spawn at the Rising City Criminal Base and gear up at the adjacent Gun Shop.
* **Step 2**: Hit the **Jewelry Store** as soon as the lights activate. Parachute off the roof.
* **Step 3**: Drive directly across town to the **Rising City Bank** if you possess a keycard.
* **Step 4**: Swing south into the **Donut Shop** for a quick cash register grab and healing supplies.
* **Step 5**: Drop off goods at the Volcano Base, collecting your bonus earnings.

### Route B: The High-Yield Heavy Rotation (Squad Recommended)

* **Step 1**: Assemble at the **Power Plant**. Solve the puzzle instantly and deploy a Volt Bike or Roadster.
* **Step 2**: Speed directly through the train tunnel bypass to deliver the uranium to the Volcano Base before the value decays.
* **Step 3**: Rally your squad at **The Museum**, solve the dual-lever puzzle, and load up your backpacks.
* **Step 4**: Fly straight to the desert to crack open **The Tomb** while the Museum drop cools down.
* **Step 5**: Finish the circuit by launching an amphibious assault on the **Oil Rig**.

## Police Strategy: Map Control and Chokepoints

Playing as law enforcement requires an entirely different understanding of the **jailbreakgame map**. Instead of pathfinding escape routes, officers must establish map control over mandatory chokepoints.

### Key Tactical Chokepoints

1. **The Volcano Base Entrance**: Because the Power Plant and Museum require criminals to visit the Volcano Base, setting up road spikes right outside the tunnel entrance guarantees easy arrests.
2. **Rising City Bridge Crossings**: The suspension bridges connecting Rising City to the mainland funnel escaping outlaws into narrow lanes, making PIT maneuvers devastatingly effective.
3. **Prison Perimeter Camping**: Guarding the sewer exits and the exterior wall behind the Prison Gun Shop prevents escaping prisoners from ever reaching their first vehicle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the largest city on the jailbreakgame map?
Rising City remains the densest metropolitan area in terms of core heists and street grids, though Crown City rivals it in vertical building height, luxury accommodations, and modern infrastructure.

### Where is the fastest vehicle spawner on the jailbreakgame map?
Top-tier supercars and hypercars are primarily found at the **1M Dealership** located near Mountainside Town, as well as select specialized pads in Rising City and the luxury penthouses of Crown City.

### How do I quickly learn the jailbreakgame map layout as a beginner?
Community reports suggest starting with aerial vehicles like the Little Bird helicopter. Flying high above the terrain gives you an unobstructed bird's-eye view of how major highways, train tracks, and waterways connect the Prison directly to Rising City, Mountainside Town, and the desert outskirts.

### Where is the best place to cash in stolen museum artifacts?
All stolen artifacts from the Museum heist must be brought to either the **Volcano Criminal Base** or the secondary drop-off point at the **Rising City Criminal Base** to convert your contraband into spendable cash.
