## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens to the map after jailbreakgame events end?
Most live updates leave behind permanent terrain upgrades. For example, dramatic events have historically introduced redesigned bases, brand-new islands, Crater City expansions, and rebalanced roadway systems that stay in the game long after the initial event timer expires.

### Can I unlock event vehicles after the season closes?
Once an event season concludes, its exclusive Tier 10 vehicle can no longer be acquired directly through gameplay. However, players can obtain past rewards via the Trade Island feature, exchanging duplicate rare cars, textures, and cosmetics with other collectors.

### How do I know when new jailbreakgame events are scheduled?
Developers typically add an in-game digital countdown billboard above high-traffic areas, such as the City Bank or the central prison yard, roughly one week prior to the spectacle. Following official development announcements and community forums will also alert you to upcoming dates.

### Do active events disable the standard police versus criminal heists?
Usually, heists stay active, but environmental conditions—such as falling meteors, rising water levels, or electrical storms—may temporarily disable specific robbery mechanics or alter escape routes, forcing both teams to adjust their tactics.
