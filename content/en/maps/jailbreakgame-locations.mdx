## The Prison Complex: Escape Routes and Hotspots

Every journey as a prisoner begins behind bars, making the prison complex one of the most active hubs on the server. To reach the rank of criminal, you must break past the outer perimeter while dodging patrolling officers.

Over the years, developers Badimo have continually refined the prison layout, adding subterranean passages, ventilation shafts, and explosive weak points. Staying alert to police patrol routes around the security office and cafeteria gives prisoners a much higher survival rate.

* **Prison Yard:** Spawning zone for inmates featuring basketball courts and sewer access manholes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which jailbreakgame locations offer the highest robbery payouts?
The Oil Rig, Museum, The Tomb, and a fast Power Plant run provide the highest payouts per run. While the Power Plant requires solving a quick wire puzzle and racing across the map before the uranium decays, players using fast aerial or ground vehicles can consistently maximize their income.

### What are the safest jailbreakgame locations for criminals to escape police?
The Volcano Criminal Base and the subterranean Rising City Criminal Base are among the safest areas. Police who enter these bases risk taking heavy environmental or automated damage, preventing them from camping inside drop-off points. The extensive sewer network beneath the prison is also one of the safest escape exits for inmates.

### Where can I buy luxury and specialized vehicles?
Mid-range and muscle cars are scattered across town garages and carparks, while supercars, helicopters, and experimental vehicles are housed at the 1M Dealership in Mountainside Town, the Military Base in the desert dunes, and designated city airports.

### How do map expansions change robbery grinding routes?
Map updates that introduced Crown City, the expanded ocean, and regional heists like the Crown Jewel spread out player density. Instead of crowding Rising City, players can now run split grinding loops between the coastal metropolis and the central desert to avoid police patrols.
