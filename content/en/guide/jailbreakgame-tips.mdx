## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the best jailbreakgame tips for solo players?
Solo players should prioritize acquiring the Little Bird helicopter or the Volt Bike as early as possible. These vehicles bypass road blockades, climb mountains effortlessly, and minimize interactions with police campers. Additionally, prioritize solo-friendly robberies such as the Power Plant, Jewelry Store, and Casino, which do not strictly require a second player to open vault doors.

### How do I stop getting tasered by police officers?
Maintain mid-range spacing and avoid running in a straight line. The taser projectile has travel time and a noticeable reload penalty on a missed shot. Use jumping, rolling, and serpentine movement patterns. If an officer draws their taser, duck behind corners or drop a smoke grenade to break their line-of-sight immediately.

### What is the fastest vehicle to buy when starting out?
While high-end supercars cost upwards of a million dollars, the Model 3 and the Patrol Bike offer phenomenal early-game value. Once you reach the mid-tier cash bracket, save directly for the Roadster or Little Bird before investing in luxury supercars.

### Can criminals bypass police forcefields?
Yes. Standard firearms cannot damage players standing inside a police forcefield bubble, but the Plasma Pistol fires energy bolts designed specifically to penetrate through forcefields. Always keep a Plasma Pistol equipped in your utility slot to counter camping tactics.
