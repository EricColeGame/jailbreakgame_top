## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which jailbreakgame gamepasses should I buy first?
If you enjoy the criminal team, the **Bigger Duffel Bag** is unequivocally the best first purchase due to its direct boost to robbery payouts. If you divide your time evenly between police and criminal teams, the **Mobile Garage** provides the greatest utility by letting you spawn vehicles anywhere on the map.

### Do jailbreakgame gamepasses transfer if I play on different devices?
Yes. Because gamepasses are linked directly to your platform account, any upgrade you purchase will remain unlocked whether you play on PC, console, mobile, or tablet devices.

### Can gamepasses be shared with friends or traded in-game?
You cannot trade active passes between accounts via in-game vehicle trading systems. However, players can use the official gifting feature in the game's store menu to purchase and deliver passes directly to another player's account using Robux.

### Does the Boss Pass alert you to every single heist?
The Boss Pass notifications monitor major fixed robberies—such as the Bank, Jewelry Store, Museum, and Power Plant—alerting you the moment security systems reset. It does not actively track non-standard dynamic events like moving cargo trains or cargo planes, which operate on separate global schedules.
