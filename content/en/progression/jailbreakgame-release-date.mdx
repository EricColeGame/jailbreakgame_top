### The Transition to Seasonal XP

Early progression relied entirely on grinding cash to buy garages full of vehicles. To introduce structured goals, Badimo implemented a Seasonal Progression System:

1. **Daily & Weekly Contracts:** Criminals and police complete specific objectives (e.g., "Bust 3 Bank robberies" or "Rob the Museum without taking damage").
2. **Season XP Tiers:** Earning XP unlocks exclusive cosmetics, textures, vehicle horns, and level 10 grand prizes.
3. **Limited Availability:** Once a season concludes, vehicles retire from regular dealership sale, entering the player-driven trading ecosystem.

For official updates and announcements straight from the creators, follow the developer team on the [Badimo official X profile](https://x.com/badimo), where new seasons, patches, and balance changes are routinely shared.
