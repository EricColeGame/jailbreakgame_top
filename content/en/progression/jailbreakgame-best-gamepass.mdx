## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which upgrade qualifies as the jailbreakgame best gamepass for beginner players?
For any new player focusing on accumulating wealth, the Duffel Bag is the **jailbreakgame best gamepass** to buy first. It immediately increases the amount of money you carry away from robberies, allowing you to afford top-tier supercars and weaponry much earlier in your playthrough.

### Does the Pro Garage gamepass let you spawn helicopters or planes?
No. The Pro Garage only permits mobile spawning of land-based ground vehicles (cars, motorcycles, and off-roaders). Aerial vehicles such as the Black Hawk, Little Bird, or Stunt Plane must still be retrieved from their fixed helipads and airports around the map.

### Is the SWAT shield bulletproof against all criminal guns?
The SWAT riot shield absorbs standard frontal bullet damage from small arms, allowing police officers to advance through hallways safely. However, it does not make the player completely invincible; explosive damage, flank attacks, and sustained high-caliber fire can still take down an officer holding the shield.

### Can I share my Duffel Bag benefits with friends in my robbery party?
No, gamepass bonuses apply exclusively to the account that purchased them. Your crew members will need their own gamepasses to carry larger quantities of stolen jewels, museum artifacts, or vault cash.
