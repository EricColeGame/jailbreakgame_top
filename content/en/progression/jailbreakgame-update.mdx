## Law Enforcement Progression: Maximizing Arrest Revenue

Playing as a police officer is one of the most underrated ways to earn steady cash, especially after a patch introduces balance passes to criminal bounties. While criminals must transport bags to secure their funds, officers receive instant cash deposits for successful arrests.

### High-Bounty Hunting Tactics

1. **Monitor the Map Feeds**: Pay attention to chat logs and visual sirens. Red robbery icons indicate active targets.
2. **Utilize Spike Strips Effectively**: Lay spikes on narrow bridges connecting the Power Plant to the Volcano.
3. **Master the Pit Maneuver**: Ramming a fleeing criminal's car temporarily disables their engine, creating a safe window for a taser shot.
4. **Secure High Ground**: Stand on top of the Bank or Museum to scan for high-bounty players before moving in.

| Tactical Tool | Best Application | Counter-Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Taser** | Halting sprinters at medium range | Jump-strafing and zig-zag sprinting |
| **Handcuffs** | Close-quarters instant arrests | Rolling away or switching to shotgun bursts |
| **Spike Strips** | Bottlenecks, tunnels, and bridge chokepoints | Flying vehicles or off-road bypass routes |
| **Roadblock Barriers**| Blocking narrow exit alleys | Driving heavy vehicles with ramming bumpers |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does a new jailbreakgame update usually go live?
Developers typically roll out major updates during weekends, often early in the morning or around midnight in US time zones. Seasonal updates arrive every few months, usually accompanied by an in-game countdown timer visible in the main lobby.

### How do robbery payouts change after a jailbreakgame update?
Payouts are balanced periodically to ensure no single robbery dominates the economy. If a particular vault or puzzle is solved too quickly by the community, developers may adjust laser patterns, lengthen cooldowns, or add new security gates to normalize hourly earnings across all activities.

### What is the fastest way to earn cash after an update drops?
The fastest method is chaining the Power Plant, Museum, and Jewelry Store while using high-acceleration vehicles like the Volt Bike or Roadster. If you own game passes like the Bigger Bag, focus heavily on high-capacity robberies like the Casino and Museum.

### Can I still trade for vehicles removed in a past jailbreakgame update?
Yes. Vehicles and cosmetics retired in previous patches can typically be traded in the dedicated Trading Hub. Their value is determined by player supply and demand, with older, limited-run seasonal vehicles demanding significant cash equivalents or rare item bundles.
